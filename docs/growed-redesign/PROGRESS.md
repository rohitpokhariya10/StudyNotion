## Capsule hero motion refinement — 6 October 2026

Layout, typography, copy, colours, navbar, CTAs and course finder unchanged. Supersedes the motion notes in the section above (including return-to-rest on leave).

- **Reference inspection** (codedemons.in, Metal-backed headless Chromium, ~60 fps timestamped frames, 1440×900; pixel analysis of eye blobs and silhouette edges): eyes slide over the curved face and foreshorten near the silhouette; silhouette left/right edges never move (no lean or sideways shift), top/bottom shift a few px (modest pitch). Eye travel ≈ −0.38…+0.34 capsule widths horizontally, −0.12 up / +0.24 down. Both characters share one mapping of the pointer's screen position with a slight rightward bias (hovering a character does not make it look straight out); pointer over its fixed header is ignored. ### Tracking follow-up — 6 October 2026
